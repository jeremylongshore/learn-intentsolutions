Common questions

FAQ.

What's the difference between the Learn page and Colab?

Learn is for self-serve resources — guides, plugins, blog posts, open source. Colab is for paid, time-boxed engagements where Jeremy works directly with your team.

Where do I find Claude Code plugins?

claudecodeplugins.io is the registry; the source repo is github.com/jeremylongshore/claude-code-plugins. Featured skills are at tonsofskills.com.

Can I hire Jeremy for a project?

Yes — engagement intake is at intentsolutions.io/colab. Active partner deliverables live behind authenticated URLs at partners.intentsolutions.io.

What if I'm not technical?

Start at intentsolutions.io/learn — the founder coaching track is designed for decision makers who don't ship code. The blog at startaitools.com also has founder-friendly entries.

How does this differ from jeremylongshore.com?

jeremylongshore.com is the portfolio site — background, talks, the personal index. learn.intentsolutions.io is the practice's learning hub — it routes you to whichever property has the resource you need.
